# Bacon Mac

|               |   |
|---------------|---|
| Rating        | 5 |
| Ease          | 2 |
| Quickness     | 3 |
| Affordability | 2 |

A from-scratch mac and cheese built on a bacon-grease roux sauce. Three cheeses shred in right before they go in the pot, so the sauce stays smooth. Most of the bacon mixes into the sauce; the rest goes on top with panko and cheese, then it all bakes until browned.

# Ingredients

|Ingredient|Amount|Notes|
|-|-|-|
|Bacon|—|Cooked in the saucepan; leaves the grease for the roux. Crumble most into the sauce, reserve some for the top|
|Flour|—|About 1–2 tbsp, to match the grease|
|Milk|—|Added slowly to the roux|
|Gouda|—|Shred it yourself|
|Sharp Cheddar|—|Shred it yourself|
|Parmesan|—|Shred it yourself; good for the topping|
|Pasta|—|Any shape|
|Panko|—|Optional topping|
|Salt and Pepper|||

# Directions

- Boil water for the pasta.
- Cook bacon in a large saucepan.
- Cook pasta.
- Shred the cheese while the bacon cooks — do not use pre-shredded cheese.
- Remove the bacon, retaining a tbsp or two of grease in the saucepan.
- Combine the grease with flour to make a roux.
- Slowly mix in milk until the sauce is slightly thinner than desired.
- Mix in the cheese, reserving some for the topping.
- Crumble most of the cooked bacon into the sauce; reserve some larger chunks or whole strips for the top.
- Mix sauce and pasta together in a baking dish.
- Add panko, the reserved cheese, and the reserved bacon as a topping.
- Bake until the cheese topping is browned — as long as you can wait.

# Other Considerations

Do not use pre-shredded cheese: the anti-caking agents make the sauce grainy. Gouda and sharp cheddar both melt well; parmesan brings the salty bite and browns nicely in the topping. If the sauce thickens too much before baking, loosen it with a splash of milk or pasta water.
