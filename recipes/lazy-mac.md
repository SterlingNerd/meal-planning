# Lazy Mac

|               |   |
|---------------|---|
| Rating        | 4 |
| Ease          | 5 |
| Quickness     | 4 |
| Affordability | 4 |

The no-roux shortcut mac and cheese: a can of unsweetened condensed milk and shredded cheese make the sauce directly, no roux. As close to boxed mac as you can get from real ingredients.

# Ingredients

|Ingredient|Amount|Notes|
|-|-|-|
|Pasta|—|Any shape|
|Condensed Milk|1 can|Unsweetened condensed / evaporated|
|Shredded Cheese|—|Cheddar or a cheddar blend; reserve some for the top if you like|
|Salt and Pepper|||

# Directions

- Cook pasta according to package directions.
- Heat the milk in a saucepan over medium.
- Add cheese to the hot milk a handful at a time until the sauce reaches the consistency you want.
- Drain the pasta and combine it with the sauce.
- Season with salt and pepper and serve.

# Other Considerations

A can of unsweetened condensed milk (evaporated milk) — sweetened condensed milk would make the whole dish taste like dessert. Pre-shredded cheese is fine here since there is no roux, but shredding your own still melts smoother. Add the cheese on low heat, or with the burner off, if the sauce starts to split.
